📜 HOW TO PLAY

1. Build a Town Hall, Gold Mine, Elixir Pump and Army Camp.
2. Select a building in your village to upgrade or collect resources.
3. Train Barbarians, Archers or Giants using elixir.
4. Tap Attack Enemy, then tap the battlefield to deploy your troops.
5. Defeat the enemy defenses to win gold and elixir. Your village saves automatically.
